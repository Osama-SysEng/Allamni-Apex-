# Curriculum: acceptance criteria

## Responsibility

The Curriculum bounded context owns standards, learning objectives, and progression maps. Every command identifies an accountable actor and correlation identifier; every sensitive side effect follows an explicit review policy.

## Learner safeguard

Adaptive recommendations are support tools, not diagnoses. Content, interventions, institution exports, and minor learner data require scoped ownership and applicable human review.

## Acceptance signal

A feature is accepted only when its test, policy contract, and operational evidence agree.
